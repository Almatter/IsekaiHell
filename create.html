06 // Gear & Assets

What does the character start with?

Equipment

One item may fill multiple equipment types. Each type adds its normal per-grade cost and the item must satisfy every type's stat requirement.

Switch Equipment

Pay 7 points once to link two equipment pieces. Only one linked piece can be active at a time, and changing the active piece uses an action.

Assets

Creation assets are capped at F. An F asset can represent something impressive, but the more improbable the starting possession, the more limited, damaged, disgraced, remote, indebted, or otherwise compromised it should be.
